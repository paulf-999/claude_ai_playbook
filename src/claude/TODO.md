## Sorting Strategy

Items in the Pending section are sorted by: **Status** → **Effort** → **Value** → **Priority**

This ordering prioritizes active work, then surfaces quick wins (low-effort items), and within each effort tier, high-impact work rises to the top:
1. **Status:** In Progress → Pending → Planning → Blocked
2. **Effort:** Low (🟢) → Medium (🟡) → High (🔴)
3. **Value:** High (💎) → Medium (💰) → Low (🟢)
4. **Priority:** High (🔴) → Medium (🟡) → Low (🟢)

**Result:** In Progress items first, then all achievable (low-effort) work becomes visible, and within each effort tier, high-value items surface naturally—ensuring high-impact work that isn't heavy effort rises to the top.
